# Choose YouTube Starter

Offer only three numbered frames to a beginner:

1. Research competitors only.
2. Research plus content.
3. Research through voice.
Attach one measurable result to each frame: research report, finished script, or usable MP3. Do not show full production as an equal first-run option. It remains available only when the customer explicitly asks for it or after smaller pilots succeed. Explain input and result without tool IDs or graph terminology. Preserve manual approval points. When the customer is satisfied, add the next requested capability and let the core connect required predecessors. Never force all eight steps at once.
